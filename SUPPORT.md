# Support

NanoGig is an unofficial, hobby, single-maintainer project. Support is best-effort with no
response-time guarantees.

- **Bugs and questions:** open a [GitHub issue](https://github.com/johnnyborjomi/NanoGig/issues).
  For anything protocol-related, attach the hex log (Menu → Log → copy) and your firmware
  version (Menu shows it). Scrub anything you consider personal first.
- **Firmware changes:** the pedal's Bluetooth protocol is reverse-engineered and verified on
  NanOS 2.2.x only. A firmware update can break decoding silently; a log from the new
  firmware is the fastest way to get it fixed.
- **Not affiliated with or endorsed by Neural DSP.** For problems with the pedal itself, use
  Neural DSP's own support.
